ABSTRACT (原文摘要，逐句来自正文):
============================================================
We propose RoboFlamingo, built upon the open-source VLM OpenFlamingo. Unlike prior works,
RoboFlamingo utilizes pre-trained VLMs for single-step vision-language comprehension, models
sequential history information with an explicit policy head, and is slightly fine-tuned by
imitation learning on language-conditioned manipulation datasets. This decomposition requires
only a minimal amount of data to adapt to downstream manipulation tasks, and offers
flexibility for open-loop control and deployment on low-performance platforms. Benefiting from
vision-language pretraining, RoboFlamingo achieves state-of-the-art performance with a large
margin and generalizes well to zero-shot settings and environments.

- Enriched 指令设定：Ours 0.720/0.480/0.299/0.211/0.144，Avg.Len 1.85；Ours(freeze-emb)
  0.737/0.530/0.385/0.275/0.192，Avg.Len 2.12；HULC 1.82；RT-1 0.86。
- 结构：总参数/LLM 参数/可训练参数 —— M-3b(MPT) 3B/1B/1B；G-4b(GPT-NeoX) 4B/3B/1B；
  L-9b(LLaMA) 9B/7B/1B；M-9b(MPT)。即只训练 ~1B 的策略头与适配层，VLM 主体基本冻结。
- 全量 CALVIN 数据下模型规模消融 Avg.Len：M-3b 3.94 → M-3b-IFT（指令微调）4.09；
  G-4b 3.67 → G-4b-IFT 3.79；L-9b 2.79；M-9b 3.97。小模型与大模型竞争力相当。
- 数据稀缺时规模效应反转：仅用 10% 语言标注数据（约为全量数据的 0.1%），Avg.Len 为
  M-3b 0.05、M-3b-IFT 0.13、G-4b 0.48、G-4b-IFT ...，大模型显著更强。
- 视觉语言能力对照：M-3b COCO 77.3 / VQAv2 45.8；M-3b-IFT 82.7/45.7；G-4b 81.8/49.0；
  M-9b 89.0/54.8。
- 冻结指令嵌入层（freeze-emb）显著缓解同义句带来的性能波动，Enriched 设定下 Avg.Len 1.85→2.12。
- 其它结论：策略头（policy head）容量不足会限制下游任务表现；VL 预训练对下游机器人任务不可或缺；
  jump step 演示数据缺失会导致性能下降，用含 jump step 的演示重训可缓解。
