ABSTRACT (原文摘要，逐句来自正文):
============================================================
The advent of LLMs has catalyzed a transformative wave in AI, paving the way foragents that use graphical user interfaces (GUIs) as a primary means of interaction. This paper presents a comprehensive survey of LLM-brained GUI agents, exploring historical evolution, core components, and advanced techniques. We address research questions on existing GUI agent frameworks, collection and utilization of data for training specialized large action models, evaluation metrics and benchmarks, and emerging applications. Through detailed analysis, this survey identifies key research gaps and outlines a roadmap for future advancements.
