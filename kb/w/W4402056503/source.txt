ABSTRACT（原文摘要，逐句来自正文）:
============================================================
Time series anomaly detection has applications in many research fields, including manufacturing
and healthcare. Anomalies indicate novel or unexpected events, such as production faults, system
defects or heart fluttering, and are of particular interest. The large size and complex patterns of
time series have led researchers to develop specialised deep learning models for detecting anomalous
patterns. This survey provides a structured and comprehensive overview of state-of-the-art deep
learning models for time series anomaly detection. It provides a taxonomy based on the factors that
divide models into categories. Besides describing the basic technique for each category, advantages
and limitations are discussed. It includes examples of deep anomaly detection across application
domains, and summarises open issues and challenges in adopting deep anomaly detection models.
