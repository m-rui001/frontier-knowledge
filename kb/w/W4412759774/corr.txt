The Original Article  was published on 30 July 2025
Correction to: Nature Biomedical Engineering https://doi.org/10.1038/s41551-025-01463-z, published online 30 July 2025.
In the version of the article initially published, in Fig. 4b and d, the light blue and light purple colours were switched in the keys and have now been corrected as seen in Fig. 1 below. In the Fig. 4f key, "GPT-4o" was light blue and has been corrected to light purple and "CRISPR-GPT" was light purple and has been corrected to green. These errors has been corrected in the HTML and PDF versions of the article.
