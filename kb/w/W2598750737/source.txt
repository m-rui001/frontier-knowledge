ABSTRACT（原文摘要，逐句来自正文）:
============================================================
Inference using significance testing and Bayes factors is compared and contrasted in five case
studies based on real research. The first illustrates that the methods often agree, both in
supporting H1 over H0 and the reverse. The next four show they often disagree. In these cases, the
paper motivates the sensible evidential conclusion, then sees which approach matches. It is shown
that a high-powered non-significant result is consistent with no evidence for H0 over H1 worth
mentioning (which a Bayes factor can show); a low-powered non-significant result can be substantial
evidence for H0 over H1; a high-powered significant result may amount to no evidence for H1 over
H0; and different theories can be evidentially supported to different degrees by the same data (a
fact p-values cannot reflect but Bayes factors can). It is argued that appropriate conclusions match
Bayesian inferences, not significance testing, where they disagree.

KEY CONTENT（正文，出处层级：论文正文）:
============================================================
- 主题：显著性检验（p 值）与贝叶斯因子（Bayes factor）的推断对比，用五个真实研究案例。
- 案例一：两法常一致（都支持 H1 或都支持 H0）。
- 案例二：高功效的不显著结果——贝叶斯因子显示几乎没有支持 H0 的证据（p 值会误读成
  "没有效应"）。
- 案例三：低功效的不显著结果——可能是支持 H0 的实质证据（贝叶斯能显示，p 值不能）。
- 案例四：高功效的显著结果——可能并不构成支持 H1 的证据（与贝叶斯结论一致）。
- 案例五：同一数据对不同理论有不同程度的支持——p 值反映不了，贝叶斯因子能。
- 结论：两法分歧时，合理的证据结论与贝叶斯推断相符，而非显著性检验。
- 语境：心理学可重复性危机背景下的统计方法争论。
