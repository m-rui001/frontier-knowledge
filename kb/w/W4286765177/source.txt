ABSTRACT（原文摘要，逐句来自正文）:
============================================================
Assessing the role of anthropogenic warming from temporally inhomogeneous historical data in the
presence of large natural variability is difficult and has caused conflicting conclusions on
detection and attribution of tropical cyclone (TC) trends. Here, using a reconstructed long-term
proxy of annual TC numbers together with high-resolution climate model experiments, we show robust
declining trends in the annual number of TCs at global and regional scales during the twentieth
century. The Twentieth Century Reanalysis (20CR) dataset is used for reconstruction because,
compared with other reanalyses, it assimilates only sea-level pressure fields rather than all
available observations, making it less sensitive to the inhomogeneous observing system.

KEY CONTENT（正文，出处层级：论文正文）:
============================================================
- 难题：历史观测在时间上不均匀、自然变率大，导致热带气旋（TC）趋势的检测与归因结论冲突。
- 方法：重建的长期年 TC 数代理指标 + 高分辨率气候模式试验。
- 关键数据源：20CR（Twentieth Century Reanalysis），只同化海平面气压场而非所有可用观测，
  对观测系统的不均匀性不敏感——这是它相对其它再分析资料的优势。
- 结论：二十世纪全球与区域尺度的年 TC 数呈稳健下降趋势。
- 语境：论文 2023-11-20 在线发表；与"变暖导致 TC 更强/更频繁"的常见叙事形成对照——
  频率下降与强度变化是两个不同问题。
