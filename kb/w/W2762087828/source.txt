ABSTRACT（原文摘要，逐句来自正文）:
============================================================
Cyclical heat engines are a paradigm of classical thermodynamics but are impractical for
miniaturization because they rely on moving parts. Particle-exchange (PE) heat engines use energy
filtering to control a thermally driven particle flow between two reservoirs. Because they do not
require moving parts and can be realized in solid-state materials, PE engines are suitable for
low-power applications and miniaturization. Here we realize a PE heat engine based on a quantum dot
and demonstrate that it operates close to thermodynamic efficiency limits. At maximum power, the
efficiency agrees with the Curzon-Ahlborn efficiency; the overall maximum efficiency exceeds 70% of
the Carnot efficiency.

KEY NUMBERS（正文，出处层级：论文正文）:
============================================================
- 引擎类型：粒子交换（particle-exchange）热机，量子点（quantum dot）实现；无运动部件、
  固态、适合小型化与低功率应用。
- 极限：Carnot 效率 ηC=(TH−TC)/TH 是上限；实际通常在最大功率处优化效率，
  接近较低的 Curzon-Ahlborn 效率 ηCA。
- 结果：最大功率条件下电子效率符合 Curzon-Ahlborn；整体最高效率超过 Carnot 的 70%。
- 参数：量子点充电能 4.9 meV，远大于最高电子温度 2 K 下的 kT=0.17 meV（无激发态输运）；
  用高质量外延量子点 + 非侵入式测温技术实现。
- 方法：测量电子热流确定电子效率。
