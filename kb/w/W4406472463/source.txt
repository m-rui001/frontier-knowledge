ABSTRACT（原文摘要，逐句来自正文）:
============================================================
The design of functional materials with desired properties is essential for energy storage,
catalysis and carbon capture. Generative models accelerate materials design by directly generating
new materials given property constraints, but current methods have a low success rate in proposing
stable crystals or satisfy only a limited set of constraints. We present MatterGen, a model that
generates stable, diverse inorganic materials across the periodic table and can be fine-tuned to
steer generation towards a broad range of property constraints. Compared with previous generative
models, structures produced by MatterGen are more than twice as likely to be new and stable, and
more than ten times closer to the local energy minimum. After fine-tuning, MatterGen generates
stable, new materials with desired chemistry, symmetry and mechanical, electronic and magnetic
properties. As a proof of concept, we synthesize one generated structure and measure its property
value to be within 20% of our target.

FULL TEXT KEY NUMBERS（正文，出处层级：论文正文）:
============================================================
- 方法：MatterGen，基于扩散的生成模型；联合扩散原子类型(atom types)、坐标(coordinates)、
  周期性晶格(periodic lattice)——逐步精化生成晶体结构。引入 adapter 模块，支持按目标化学组成、
  对称性、标量性质微调。
- SUN 指标：Stable、Unique、Novel（稳定/唯一/新颖）；用 1024 个 MatterGen 样本、1000 个基线样本计算。
- 对照：DiffCSP、CDVAE、P-G-SchNet、G-SchNet、FTCP；MatterGen-MP 是只在 MP-20（较小数据集）
  上训练的版本，用来与同数据下的基线公平比较。
- 结果：相比 CDVAE 与 DiffCSP，MatterGen-MP 生成的 SUN 结构多 60%，生成结构到 DFT 弛豫后
  的平均 RMSD 低 50%。整体（大数据集版）比先前模型：新且稳定的可能性超 2 倍，
  距局部能量最小点近超 10 倍。
- 微调能力：按化学组成、对称性、力学（体模量 target 50/100/150/200 GPa）、电子、磁学性质生成。
  过滤流程：唯一性/新颖性 → MatterSim+DFT 的 energy-above-hull 稳定性 → MatterSim 声子稳定性
  → 是否含氧。
- 实验验证：合成一个生成结构，实测性质在目标值 20% 以内（proof of concept）。
- 意义：向材料设计的基础生成模型迈进一步。
