ABSTRACT（原文摘要，逐句来自正文）:
============================================================
The Fröhlich electron-phonon interaction, which dominates carrier scattering in polar materials at
long wavelengths, is usually treated with model parameters. We present a first-principles approach
to compute the Fröhlich vertex without empirical parameters, by separating the long-range part
(computed from the Born effective charges and the dielectric tensor) from the short-range part
(computed on a dense Brillouin-zone grid). An interpolation scheme combines the two. Applied to
anatase TiO2, the method reveals strong anisotropy and gives carrier lifetimes and scattering rates
in agreement with experiment.

KEY NUMBERS / CONTENT（正文，出处层级：论文正文）:
============================================================
- 方法：把 Fröhlich 电声顶点分解为长程部分（由玻恩有效电荷与介电张量解析给出）与短程部分
  （在密集布里渊区网格上算），用四步插值方案合并；无需经验参数。
- 应用：锐钛矿二氧化钛（anatase TiO2）。
- 数字：512000 个波矢网格；能量分辨 10 meV；温度 20 K；声子能量阈值 90/40 meV；
  载流子寿命 3–9 fs；三倍各向异性；标准插值方法高估约一个数量级。
- 意义：第一性原理地处理极性材料里支配载流子散射的长程 Fröhlich 相互作用。
