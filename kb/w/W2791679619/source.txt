ABSTRACT（原文摘要，逐句来自正文）:
============================================================
Owing to its longevity and enormous information density, DNA has emerged as a promising archival
storage medium. However, due to technological constraints, data can only be written onto many short
DNA molecules stored in an unordered way, and can only be read by sampling from this DNA pool.
Moreover, imperfections in writing (synthesis), reading (sequencing), storage and handling,
particularly amplification via PCR, lead to loss of DNA molecules and induce errors within molecules.
To design DNA storage systems, a qualitative and quantitative understanding of errors and molecule
loss is crucial. Here we characterize those error probabilities by analyzing data from our own
experiments as well as from two other groups. We find that errors within molecules are mainly due
to synthesis and sequencing, while imperfections in handling and storage lead to significant loss of
sequences. The aim is to guide the design of future DNA data storage systems by providing a
quantitative and qualitative understanding of the DNA data storage channel.

KEY CONTENT（正文，出处层级：论文正文）:
============================================================
- DNA 作为归档介质：寿命长、信息密度巨大。
- 约束：只能写短链（难以合成显著长于常规长度的 DNA）；分子无序存放；只能采样读取（coupon
  collector 问题，需要提高读取覆盖度）。
- 信道模型：由采样分布 + 插入/删除/替换（insertion/deletion/substitution）的分布决定，
  决定能存多少数据。
- PCR 扩增：每轮每分子复制约 1.6–1.8 倍，比例依合成方法、PCR 步骤、存储衰减而变。
- 错误来源：分子内错误主要来自合成与测序；操作与存储的缺陷导致序列大量丢失。
- 具体：同聚物（homopolymer）长度超过六个时替换与删除错误率显著上升；高 GC 含量分子
  丢失率高；现有纠错方案加 5–10 核苷酸可纠 2–3 个替换错误。
- 方法：分析自己及两个其他组实验数据，给出 DNA 数据存储信道的定量刻画。
