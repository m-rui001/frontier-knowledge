TITLE: Purinergic receptor P2RY12-dependent microglial closure of the injured blood–brain barrier

ABSTRACT（原文摘要，逐句来自正文）:
============================================================
Microglia are integral functional elements of the central nervous system, but their contribution to
the structural integrity of the neurovascular unit has not been assessed. We show that following
blood–brain barrier (BBB) breakdown, P2RY12-mediated chemotaxis of microglial processes is required
for rapid closure of the BBB. Mice treated with the P2RY12 inhibitor clopidogrel, as well as those
in which P2RY12 was genetically ablated, exhibited significantly diminished movement of juxtavascular
microglial processes and failed to close laser-induced openings of the BBB. Thus, microglial cells
play a previously unrecognized protective role in maintaining BBB integrity following cerebrovascular
damage. Because clopidogrel antagonizes the platelet P2Y12 receptor and is widely prescribed for
coronary and cerebrovascular disease, the findings suggest caution in postincident continuation of
P2RY12-targeted platelet inhibition.

KEY CONTENT（正文，出处层级：论文正文）:
============================================================
- 对象：小胶质细胞（microglia）对血脑屏障（BBB）结构完整性的贡献。
- 发现：BBB 破损后，P2RY12 介导的小胶质细胞突起趋化是快速封闭 BBB 所必需的。
- 实验：激光诱导单毛细血管损伤（软膜下 80–150 µm）；光消融小胶质细胞使 BBB 渗漏无法封闭；
  抑制 P2RY12 或基因敲除使突起运动减少、BBB 封闭延迟。
- 药物关联：氯吡格雷（clopidogrel）是 P2Y12 拮抗剂、广泛用于冠心病与脑血管病；本研究提示
  事件后继续 P2RY12 靶向的血小板抑制需谨慎。
- 意义：揭示小胶质细胞在脑血管损伤后维持 BBB 的完整、此前未被认识的保护作用。
