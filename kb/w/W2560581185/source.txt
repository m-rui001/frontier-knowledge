ABSTRACT（原文摘要，逐句来自正文）:
============================================================
The concept of randomness plays an important role in many disciplines. On one hand, whether random
processes exist is fundamental to our understanding of nature. On the other hand, randomness is a
resource for cryptography, algorithms and simulations. Standard methods for generating randomness
rely on assumptions about the devices that are difficult to meet in practice. However, quantum
technologies allow new methods for generating certified randomness. These methods are known as
device-independent because they do not rely on any modeling of the devices. Here we review the
efforts and challenges in designing device-independent randomness generators.

KEY CONTENT（正文，出处层级：论文正文）:
============================================================
- 随机性的双重角色：理解自然的基础问题；密码学/算法/模拟的资源。
- 传统随机数生成的软肋：依赖对发生器件的建模假设，实践中难以满足。
- 量子方案：设备无关（device-independent）的认证随机性生成器——不依赖对器件的任何建模，
  仅从观测到的统计（如贝尔不等式违背）就能认证随机性。
- 综述：梳理设备无关随机性的设计努力与挑战。
- 意义：把随机性从"相信器件在产生随机"变成"可数学认证"，对密码学尤其重要。
