ABSTRACT（原文摘要，逐句来自正文）:
============================================================
Lifted Kramers spin degeneracy (LKSD) has been a central topic of condensed-matter physics since
the dawn of band theory. It underpins applications from magnetic-memory technology to topological
quantum matter. Traditionally LKSD was considered to originate from two internal symmetry-breaking
mechanisms: (1) time-reversal symmetry breaking by magnetization of ferromagnets (strong, from
non-relativistic exchange); (2) crystals with broken inversion symmetry (weaker, from relativistic
spin-orbit coupling SOC). A recent theory work based on spin-symmetry classification identified an
unconventional magnetic phase, altermagnetic, allowing LKSD without net magnetization and without
inversion-symmetry breaking. Here we provide confirmation using photoemission spectroscopy and
ab initio calculations. We identify two distinct unconventional mechanisms of LKSD generated by
the altermagnetic phase of centrosymmetric MnTe with vanishing net magnetization. Our observation
motivates exploration of this phase in insulators, semiconductors, metals and superconductors
previously perceived as conventional antiferromagnets.

FULL TEXT KEY CONTENT（正文，出处层级：论文正文）:
============================================================
- 背景：LKSD（解除 Kramers 自旋简并）传统上两个机制——铁磁体磁化的时间反演破缺（强，交换起源）；
  反演破缺晶体的 SOC（较弱）。近年自旋群分类提出第三类磁相：altermagnetic（交替磁性），
  无净磁化+无反演破缺仍能 LKSD。
- 实验：ARPES（角分辨光电子谱），15 K，软 X 射线 667 eV，MnTe(0001) 薄膜由 MBE 生长在
  InP(111)A 单晶上；测量在 k_z=0 节点平面内。
- 两个机制：
  * weak altermagnetic LKSD：非相对论能带里偶数个自旋简并节点面穿过 Γ 点，SOC 能提升简并
    即使不破反演对称。由 SOC 产生，~100 meV 尺度，与 BiTeI 等非中心对称重元素晶体的
    相对论自旋分裂记录值相当。
  * strong altermagnetic LKSD：在 k_z≈0.35 Å^-1 沿 Γ-M 路径，交换起源、更强（非相对论交换驱动）。
- 关键特征：偶动量（even-in-momentum）自旋分裂；Γ 点附近极端二次（quadratic）能带色散与
  自旋分裂，实验与理论一致。
- 意义：在曾被视为传统反铁磁体的一大族材料（绝缘体、半导体、金属、超导体）中开辟
  交替磁性探索，指向自旋电子学新器件（无净磁化的磁存储器，避免铁磁体的容量/速度限制）。
