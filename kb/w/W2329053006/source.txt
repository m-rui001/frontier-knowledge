ABSTRACT（原文摘要，逐句来自正文）:
============================================================
Compass models are theories of matter in which the couplings between internal spin (or other
relevant field) components are inherently spatially (direction) dependent. A simple illustrative
example is the 90-degree compass model on a square lattice in which only couplings of the form
tau_i^x tau_j^x are associated with nearest neighbors separated along the x axis, while tau_i^y tau_j^y
couplings appear for neighbors along the y axis. Such compass-type interactions appear in diverse
physical systems including Mott insulators with strong spin-orbit coupling, transition metal oxides,
and Josephson junction arrays. This review covers the theory and physical motivations of compass
and Kitaev models.

KEY NUMBERS / CONTENT（正文，出处层级：论文正文）:
============================================================
- 模型：90° 罗盘模型（方格子，x 方向链结 τxτx、y 方向 τyτy）；更一般 compass 与 Kitaev 模型。
- 中间对称性与后果：维数约化、平带、指数简并、临界温度序列。
- 临界温度数据：0.4048J / 0.147±0.001J / 0.14612J（经典），量子约 0.0585J。
- 现象：无序生序（order by disorder）、平带新定理。
- 物理载体：强自旋轨道耦合的 Mott 绝缘体、过渡金属氧化物、约瑟夫森结阵列；基塔耶夫蜂窝模型与铱酸盐（iridates）对接。
