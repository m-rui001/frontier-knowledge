ABSTRACT（原文摘要，逐句来自正文）:
============================================================
A quantum internet is the holy grail of quantum information processing, enabling deployment of
a broad range of quantum technologies and protocols on a global scale. However, numerous challenges
exist before it can become a reality. Perhaps the most crucial is the realization of a quantum
repeater, an essential component in long-distance transmission of quantum information. As the analog
of a classical repeater, extender or booster, the quantum repeater overcomes loss and noise in the
quantum channels comprising a network. Here we review the conceptual frameworks and architectures
for quantum repeaters, as well as experimental progress towards their realization. We also discuss
near-term proposals to overcome communication-rate limits set by point-to-point quantum
communication, and overview how quantum repeaters fit within the broader challenge of designing
and implementing a quantum internet.
