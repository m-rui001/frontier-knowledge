ABSTRACT（原文摘要，逐句来自正文）:
============================================================
Silicon photonics has developed into a mainstream technology driven by advances in optical
communications. The current generation has led to a proliferation of integrated photonic devices
from thousands to millions, mainly as communication transceivers for data centers. Products in
sensing and computing are around the corner. What will it take to increase silicon photonics from
millions to billions of units shipped? What will the next generation look like? What are the
common threads in the integration and fabrication bottlenecks, and which emerging technologies can
solve them? This perspective charts generational trends, drawing parallels from CMOS generational
definitions. It identifies crucial challenges for CMOS-foundry-compatible devices, circuits,
integration and packaging, and challenges critical to next-generation systems and applications in
communication, signal processing and sensing.

FULL TEXT KEY CONTENT（正文，出处层级：论文正文）:
============================================================
- 路线图（借鉴 CMOS 代数定义）：PIC 于 1985 引入，SOI 低损耗波导 1991–92；随后进入
  SSI 时代（1–10 器件/PIC，高速 pn 结调制器、光电探测器、III-V 激光器异质集成）。
  MSI 时代（10–500 器件）：Mach-Zehnder 调制器（MZM）在数据中心 IMDD 收发器中商用成功，
  单波长与多波长。LSI 时代（500–10,000 器件）：LIDAR、图像投影、光交换、光计算、
  可编程电路、多路生物传感器；VLSI（>10,000 器件）原型已演示。
- 目标：从百万台到十亿台出货。关键挑战：CMOS 代工厂兼容的器件、电路、集成、封装；
  通信、信号处理、传感三领域系统级挑战。
- 集成瓶颈：晶圆级测试（grating coupler 低插入损耗但偏振/温度敏感）、激光器异质集成
  （die-to-wafer 键合用 known-good dies 提升良率；wafer-to-wafer 因 SOI 200/300mm 与
  调制器 150mm 尺寸不匹配浪费）、CTE 失配、键合良率。
- 热管理：金属加热器/掺杂波导热光效应 1–10 μs 响应，功耗大、产生热串扰，限制 LSI/VLSI 扩展。
- 能源效率指标：光接收器 0.7 pJ/bit（65nm CMOS duobinary sampling）；SiGe 雪崩接收器
  fJ/bit 能效；Ge/Si 雪崩光电二极管 340 GHz 增益带宽积。
- 应用：CPO（共封装光学）成功依赖硅光子学；高性能计算扩展、分解式计算。
- 现状：光电子与电子 IC（EIC）并存，系统层面几乎 100% 由数据通信主导。
