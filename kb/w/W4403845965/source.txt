ABSTRACT（原文摘要，逐句来自 Semantic Scholar 收录的官方摘要）:
============================================================
Despite increasing interest in using AI and ML models for drug development, effectively
interpreting their predictions remains a challenge, limiting their impact on clinical decisions.
We address this by providing a practical guide to SHapley Additive exPlanations (SHAP), a popular
feature-based interpretability method that can be integrated into supervised ML models to gain
deeper understanding of predictions, enhancing transparency and trustworthiness. This tutorial
focuses on applying SHAP to standard ML black-box models for regression and classification. We
provide an overview of various visualization plots and their interpretation, available software
for implementing SHAP, and highlight best practices and special considerations when dealing with
binary endpoints and time-series models. We also apply it to inherently explainable regression
models. Finally, we discuss limitations and ongoing advancements addressing current drawbacks.
