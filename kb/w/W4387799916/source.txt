ABSTRACT (原文摘要，逐句来自正文):
============================================================
Careful choices in formatting few-shot examples during in-context learning introduce spurious biases that may lead to significantly different conclusions in model performance. The sensitivity to formatting choices that we discover across widely-used open-source models suggests that future research would benefit from reporting a performance spread over reasonable variation in prompt formats, in addition to mean performance. We focus on LLM sensitivity to a quintessential class of meaning-preserving design choices: prompt formatting. Several widely used open-source LLMs are extremely sensitive to subtle changes in prompt formatting in few-shot settings, with performance differences of up to 70 accuracy points across formats and models. Results on tasks can be reversed just by choosing different formats.

FULL TEXT KEY NUMBERS (arXiv HTML v1 正文，出处层级：论文正文):
============================================================
- 关注：few-shot上下文学习中"保持语义"的提示格式选择(格式、标点、示例排列等)引入的虚假特征(spurious features)。
- 关键数字：格式选择导致性能差异最高超70个准确率点;20%的任务在所有LLaMA-2设置下spread≥15准确率点,所有Falcon设置下≥9点;有的任务结论会因格式不同而被逆转(reversed)。
- 方法：在"非对抗用户可能合理选择"的格式空间上评估;提出FormatSpread——快速探索提示格式空间的方法(受梯度无关prompt engineering工作启发),避免线性增加n个格式的全量评估成本。
- 发现：4.3小提示变化导致大性能差异;4.4提示格式是prompt embedding的可识别变换;4.5 FormatSpread。
- 任务含:把段落分入性别/职业/种族/宗教四类刻板印象或反刻板印象。
- 建议:报告模型性能时除均值外,应报告合理格式变化下的性能spread。
