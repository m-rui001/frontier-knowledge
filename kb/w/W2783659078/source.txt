TITLE: Boron nitride nanoresonators for phonon-enhanced molecular vibrational spectroscopy at the strong coupling limit

ABSTRACT（原文摘要，逐句来自正文）:
============================================================
Enhanced light-matter interactions are the basis of surface-enhanced infrared absorption (SEIRA)
spectroscopy, which conventionally relies on plasmonic materials focusing light to nanoscale spots.
Phonon polariton nanoresonators made of polar crystals are an alternative, exhibiting quality factors
far beyond plasmonic counterparts. The emergence of van der Waals crystals enables high-quality
nanophotonic resonators based on phonon polaritons, as reported for hexagonal boron nitride (h-BN).
Here we use phonon-polariton-resonant h-BN ribbons for SEIRA spectroscopy of small amounts of
organic molecules in Fourier transform infrared spectroscopy. Strikingly, the interaction between
phonon polaritons and molecular vibrations experimentally reaches the onset of the strong coupling
regime, while numerical simulations predict vibrational coupling at the level of a few molecules.

KEY NUMBERS（正文，出处层级：论文正文）:
============================================================
- 技术：表面增强红外吸收（SEIRA）光谱，传统用等离激元材料把光聚焦到纳米尺度。
- 替代方案：极性晶体的声子极化激元纳米谐振器，品质因数（Q）远高于等离激元。
- 材料：六方氮化硼（h-BN），范德华晶体；h-BN 纳米锥/棒此前已实现 Q 高达 283 的窄共振。
- 结果：h-BN 天线 Q≈230，金天线 Q≈4——高出近两个数量级；实验中声子极化激元与分子振动
  相互作用达到强耦合的起始；数值模拟预测可到几个分子的耦合水平。
- 方法：傅里叶变换红外光谱（FTIR）测少量有机分子。
