ABSTRACT（原文摘要，逐句来自正文）:
============================================================
This paper presents a comprehensive and practical guide for practitioners and end-users working
with LLMs in downstream NLP tasks. We provide discussions and insights from the perspectives of
models, data, and downstream tasks. We introduce and summarize current GPT-style and BERT-style
LLMs, discuss the influence of pre-training data, training data and test data. Most importantly,
we provide a detailed discussion about the use and non-use cases of LLMs for various NLP tasks,
such as knowledge-intensive tasks, traditional NLU tasks, natural language generation tasks,
emergent abilities, and task-specific considerations. We present use cases and non-use cases to
illustrate practical applications and limitations in real-world scenarios. We also explore the
impact of spurious biases on LLMs and delve into efficiency, cost and latency for LLM deployment.
A curated list of resources: github.com/Mooler0410/LLMsPracticalGuide.
