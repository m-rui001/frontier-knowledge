ABSTRACT（原文摘要，逐句来自 OpenAlex 倒排索引还原）:
============================================================
GPT-3.5 and GPT-4 are the two most widely used LLM services. However, when and how these models
are updated over time is opaque. We evaluate the March 2023 and June 2023 versions of GPT-3.5 and
GPT-4 on several diverse tasks: 1) math problems, 2) sensitive/dangerous questions, 3) opinion
surveys, 4) multi-hop knowledge-intensive questions, 5) generating code, 6) US Medical License
tests, and 7) visual reasoning. We find that the performance and behavior of both GPT-3.5 and
GPT-4 can vary greatly over time. For example, GPT-4 (March 2023) was reasonable at identifying
prime vs. composite numbers (84% accuracy) but GPT-4 (June 2023) was poor on these same questions
(51% accuracy). This is partly explained by a drop in GPT-4's amenity to follow chain-of-thought
prompting. GPT-3.5 was much better in June than in March on this task. GPT-4 became less willing
to answer sensitive questions and opinion survey questions in June than in March. GPT-4 performed
better at multi-hop questions in June than in March, while GPT-3.5's performance dropped.
Both had more formatting mistakes in code generation in June than in March. We provide evidence
that GPT-4's ability to follow user instructions has decreased over time. The behavior of the
"same" LLM service can change substantially in a short time, highlighting the need for continuous
monitoring of LLMs.

可确认的事实（来自摘要，出处层级：原文摘要）:
============================================================
- 被测版本：GPT-3.5 与 GPT-4 的 2023 年 3 月版与 6 月版。
- 七类任务：数学题、敏感/危险问题、观点调查、多跳知识密集问题、代码生成、美国医师执照考试、
  视觉推理。
- 具体数字：质数/合数判断，GPT-4(3月) 84% → GPT-4(6月) 51%；GPT-3.5 同任务 6 月远好于 3 月。
- 变化方向：GPT-4 在 6 月更不愿回答敏感问题与观点调查；多跳问题更好；代码格式错误更多。
  GPT-3.5 多跳问题下降。
- 解释：GPT-4 遵循思维链提示的意愿下降，是多项漂移的部分原因；作者提供证据表明
  GPT-4 遵循用户指令的能力随时间下降。
- 结论：同一个 LLM 服务的行为能在短时间内大幅改变，需要持续监测。
