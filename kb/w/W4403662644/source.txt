ABSTRACT (原文摘要，逐句来自正文):
============================================================
We present Show-o, a unified transformer that unifies multimodal understanding and generation. Unlike fully autoregressive models, Show-o unifies autoregressive and (discrete) diffusion modeling to adaptively handle inputs and outputs of various and mixed modalities. The unified model flexibly supports a wide range of vision-language tasks including visual question-answering, text-to-image generation, text-guided inpainting/extrapolation, and mixed-modality generation. Across various benchmarks, it demonstrates comparable or superior performance to existing individual models with an equivalent or larger number of parameters.

FULL TEXT KEY CONTENT (arXiv HTML v1 正文，出处层级：论文正文表格):
============================================================
- 核心：单个Transformer统一多模态理解与生成;不同于全自回归模型,统一自回归(文本token)与离散扩散(图像)建模,自适应处理不同/混合模态的输入输出。
- 支持任务：视觉问答、文生图、文本引导的修复/外推(inpainting/extrapolation)、混合模态生成。
- Tokenization：图像离散化;文本自回归建模,图像patch/像素用离散扩散。
- 训练：统一训练流水线。
- 评测：理解用POPE/MME/Flickr30k/VQAv2/GQA/MMMU;生成用MSCOCO上的FID、GenEval(按SD3设置)。
- 表格片段(理解,列POPE/MME/Flickr30k/VQAv2/GQA/MMMU)：LLaVA-v1.5 7B 85.9/1510.7/-/78.5/62.0/35.4;InstructBLIP 13B 78.9/1212.8/-/-/49.5/-;对照含理解专用模型与生成专用模型(Stable Diffusion等)。
- 结论：在相当或更少参数下,达到或超过同等规模的专用模型。
