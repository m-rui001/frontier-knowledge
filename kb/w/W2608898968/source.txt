ABSTRACT（原文摘要，逐句来自正文）:
============================================================
The process of superradiance can extract angular momentum and energy from astrophysical black holes
to populate gravitationally bound states with an exponentially large number of light bosons. We
analytically calculate superradiant growth rates for vectors around rotating black holes in the regime
where the vector Compton wavelength is much larger than the black hole size. Spin-1 bound states have
superradiance times as short as a second around stellar black holes, growing up to a thousand times
faster than their spin-0 counterparts. The fast rates allow us to use measurements of rapidly spinning
black holes in X-ray binaries to exclude a wide range of masses for weakly-coupled spin-1 particles,
5×10^-14 to 2×10^-11 eV; lighter masses in 6×10^-20 to 2×10^-17 eV start to be constrained by
supermassive black hole spin measurements at lower confidence.

KEY NUMBERS（正文，出处层级：论文正文）:
============================================================
- 机制：超辐射（superradiance）从旋转黑洞提取角动量与能量，布居引力束缚的轻玻色子态。
- 计算：转动黑洞周围矢量场（spin-1）的超辐射增长率（康普顿波长远大于黑洞尺寸的 regime）。
- 结果：spin-1 束缚态在恒星级黑洞周围超辐射时间可短至 1 秒，比 spin-0 快最多约千倍。
- 约束：用 X 射线双星中快速旋转黑洞的测量，排除弱耦合 spin-1 粒子质量 5×10^-14 到
  2×10^-11 eV；更轻的 6×10^-20 到 2×10^-17 eV 被超大质量黑洞自旋测量以较低置信度约束。
- 意义：把黑洞当成"粒子物理探测器"，用自旋测量排除新粒子的参数空间。
