ABSTRACT（原文摘要，逐句来自正文）:
============================================================
Marine heatwaves (MHWs)—periods of exceptionally warm ocean temperature lasting weeks to years—
are widely recognized for their capacity to disrupt marine ecosystems. The substantial ecological
and socioeconomic impacts present significant challenges to marine resource managers, who would
benefit from forewarning of MHWs. Despite extensive research into the physical drivers, there has
been no comprehensive global assessment of our ability to predict these events. Here we use a large
multimodel ensemble of global climate forecasts to develop and assess MHW forecasts covering the
world's oceans with lead times of up to a year. Using 30 years of retrospective forecasts, we show
that the onset, intensity and duration of MHWs are often predictable, with skilful forecasts
possible from 1 to 12 months in advance depending on region, season and the state of large-scale
climate modes such as ENSO. We discuss considerations for setting decision thresholds based on
the probability of a MHW occurring, empowering stakeholders to act based on their risk profile.

KEY CONTENT（正文，出处层级：论文正文）:
============================================================
- 对象：海洋热浪（MHW），持续数周到数年的异常暖水期，破坏海洋生态（珊瑚白化等）。
- 缺口：物理驱动研究很多，但全球性的"我们能否预测 MHW"评估此前没有。
- 方法：大型多模式集合（multimodel ensemble）的全球气候预报；30 年回报（retrospective）预报。
- 结果：MHW 的起始、强度、持续时间常常可预报；技巧（skill）随区域、季节、ENSO 等大尺度
  气候模态状态变化，提前 1–12 个月不等。
- 应用：按 MHW 发生概率设决策阈值，让利益相关方按自身风险偏好行动；指向业务化 MHW
  预报系统。
- 气候预报系统已常规用于 ENSO 预测与 NOAA 珊瑚白化展望，本文把这些系统扩展到全球 MHW 预测。
