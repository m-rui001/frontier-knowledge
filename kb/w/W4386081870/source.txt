ABSTRACT (原文摘要，逐句来自正文):
============================================================
Recent studies have shown that Large Language Models (LLMs) are capable of simulating human behavior and demonstrating remarkable abilities as autonomous agents. However, in real-world scenarios, complex tasks such as software development, consulting, and game playing might require cooperation among individuals to achieve better effectiveness. AgentVerse is a framework to facilitate multi-agent collaboration, where a group of AI agents work together to solve complex tasks. Experiments demonstrate that AgentVerse framework can effectively deploy multi-agent groups that outperform a single agent. Furthermore, we delve into the emergence of social behaviors among individual agents within a group during collaborative task accomplishment. In view of these behaviors, we discuss some possible strategies to leverage positive ones and mitigate negative ones.

FULL TEXT KEY CONTENT (arXiv HTML v1 正文，出处层级：论文正文)
============================================================
- 框架：把群体解决问题拆成四阶段：(1) Expert Recruitment（专家招募，据当前进度调整专家agent）；(2) Collaborative Decision-Making（协作决策，招募的agent讨论制定策略）；(3) Action Execution（执行）；(4) Evaluation（评估）。循环迭代。
- 通信结构：横向通信(horizontal)与纵向通信(vertical)两种；软件开发案例中采用纵向结构。
- 模型：GPT-3.5-Turbo-0613 与 GPT-4-0613。
- 任务：需要对话、数学计算、逻辑推理、编码能力的任务；案例研究含软件开发、咨询、Minecraft 游戏。
- 软件开发案例：招募三名评审角色——software engineer、UX designer、software tester；多agent组确保覆盖软件开发各方面。
- 涌现行为（Minecraft 中）：(1) volunteer behaviors（主动帮同伴，提升团队效率）；(2) conformity behaviors（在批评下调整偏离行为以对齐共同目标）；还讨论了负面行为及缓解策略。
- 实验：定量分析 AgentVerse 多agent组优于单agent；案例研究更复杂真实应用。
- 附录：定量实验配置、Minecraft 实验细节、prompts、定性实验样例。
