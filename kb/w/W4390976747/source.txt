ABSTRACT（原文摘要，逐句来自正文）:
============================================================
We introduce the refractiveindex.info database, a comprehensive open-source repository containing
optical constants for a wide array of materials, and describe the underlying dataset. This
collection, derived from meticulous compilation of data from peer-reviewed publications,
manufacturers' datasheets and authoritative texts, aims to advance research in optics and photonics.
The data is stored in a YAML-based format, ensuring integrity, consistency and ease of access.
Each record carries detailed metadata, facilitating comprehensive understanding and efficient use.
In this descriptor, we outline the data curation protocols and the file format for data records,
and briefly demonstrate how the data can be organized in a user-friendly fashion akin to books
in a traditional library.

FULL TEXT KEY CONTENT（正文，出处层级：论文正文）:
============================================================
- 数据库：refractiveindex.info，开源，收录大量材料的光学常数（折射率 n、消光系数 k 等）。
- 数据来源：同行评议文献、厂商数据表、权威书籍的细致汇编。
- 存储格式：基于 YAML，保证完整性、一致性、易访问；每条记录带详细元数据。
- 内容：数据策展协议（curation protocols）、记录文件格式；用类似传统图书馆书籍的方式组织数据，
  便于用户友好地检索使用。
- 目的：推进光学与光子学研究。
