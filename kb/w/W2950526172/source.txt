ABSTRACT（原文摘要，逐句来自正文）:
============================================================
We survey recent advances in mean estimation and regression function estimation. In particular, we
describe sub-Gaussian mean estimators for possibly heavy-tailed data, both univariate and
multivariate. We focus on estimators based on median-of-means techniques, but other methods such as
the trimmed mean and Catoni's estimator are also reviewed. We give detailed proofs for the
cornerstone results. We dedicate a section to statistical learning problems—in particular,
regression function estimation—in the presence of possibly heavy-tailed data.

KEY CONTENT（正文，出处层级：论文正文）:
============================================================
- 主题：重尾分布下的均值估计与回归函数估计。
- 核心问题：经典样本均值在重尾数据下方差大、集中性差；如何构造具有亚高斯（sub-Gaussian）
  集中性的均值估计器。
- 方法：median-of-means（分块取中位数）为中心，兼及截尾均值（trimmed mean）、Catoni 估计器。
- 覆盖：单变量与多变量设置；统计学习中的回归函数估计（重尾噪声/设计）。
- 特点：给出核心结果的详细证明（综述兼教程）。
- 关键词：mean estimation, heavy-tailed distributions, robustness, regression, statistical learning。
- 定位：理论统计/学习理论的综述，数学门槛高。
