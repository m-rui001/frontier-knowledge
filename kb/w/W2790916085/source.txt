ABSTRACT（原文摘要，逐句来自正文）:
============================================================
Human perceptual decisions are often described as optimal, but this view remains controversial. To
elucidate the issue, we review the vast literature on suboptimalities in perceptual tasks and compile
the proposed hypotheses about the origins of suboptimal behavior. Further, we argue that general
claims about optimality are virtually meaningless and result in a false sense of progress. Instead,
real progress can be achieved by building observer models that account for both optimal and
suboptimal behavior. The field should focus on assessing the hypotheses about suboptimal behavior
compiled here and stop chasing optimality.

============================================================
- 主题：人类感知决策的"最优性"之争。
- 立场：笼统地说"最优"或"次优"几乎无意义、制造虚假进步感；真正进步在于建立能同时解释
  最优与次优行为的观察者模型。
- 方法：综述感知任务中次优行为的大量文献，汇编关于次优行为起源的各种假说。
- 主张：领域应专注于检验这些次优假说，停止追逐"最优"标签。
