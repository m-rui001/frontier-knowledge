ABSTRACT（原文摘要，逐句来自正文）:
============================================================
Time-reversal symmetry (TRS) is pivotal for materials' optical, magnetic, topological and transport
properties. Chiral phonons, characterized by atoms rotating unidirectionally around their
equilibrium positions, generate dynamic lattice structures that break TRS. Here we report that
coherent chiral phonons, driven by circularly polarized terahertz light pulses, can polarize the
paramagnetic spins in CeF3 like a quasi-static magnetic field on the order of 1 Tesla. Through
time-resolved Faraday rotation and Kerr ellipticity, we found the transient magnetization is only
excited by pulses resonant with phonons, proportional to the phonon angular momentum, and growing
with magnetic susceptibility at cryogenic temperatures, as expected from the spin-phonon coupling
model. The time-dependent effective magnetic field quantitatively agrees with that calculated
from phonon dynamics. Our results may open a new route to directly investigate mode-specific
spin-phonon interactions.

KEY NUMBERS（正文，出处层级：论文正文）:
============================================================
- 核心结果：圆偏振太赫兹脉冲驱动的相干手性声子，像准静态磁场一样极化 CeF3 中的顺磁自旋，
  有效磁场约 1 Tesla 量级。
- 材料：CeF3（稀土卤化物），顺磁自旋。
- 测量：时间分辨 Faraday 旋转与 Kerr 椭圆率；瞬态磁化只由与声子共振的脉冲激发，
  与声子角动量成正比，随低温磁化率增长，符合自旋-声子耦合模型。
- 时间依赖有效磁场与声子动力学计算定量吻合。
- 意义：直接研究模式选择性的自旋-声子相互作用的新路径；手性声子打破时间反演对称，
  影响光学/磁学/拓扑/输运性质。
