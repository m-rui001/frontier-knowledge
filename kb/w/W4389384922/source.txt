ABSTRACT（原文摘要，逐句来自正文）:
============================================================
We develop a new approach to estimating causal effects of treatments or instruments that combine
multiple sources of variation according to a known formula. Examples include treatments capturing
spillovers in social or transportation networks and simulated instruments for policy eligibility.
We show how exogenous shocks to some, but not all, determinants of such variables can be leveraged
while avoiding omitted variables bias. Our solution involves specifying counterfactual shocks that
may as well have been realized and adjusting for a summary measure of non-randomness in shock
exposure: the average treatment (or instrument) across shock counterfactuals. We use this approach
to address bias when estimating employment effects of transportation infrastructure.

KEY CONTENT（正文，出处层级：论文正文）:
============================================================
- 问题：当处理变量/工具变量按已知公式组合多种变异来源时（如社会/交通网络中的溢出、
  政策资格模拟工具），如何估计因果效应。
- 洞见：只对部分（非全部）决定因素有外生冲击时也能用，同时避免遗漏变量偏误（OVB）。
- 方法：设定"本来也可能实现"的反事实冲击，用一个汇总量校正冲击暴露的非随机性——
  即跨冲击反事实的平均处理（或工具）值。
- 应用：估计交通基础设施对就业的影响（中国市场准入增长），展示如何在实践中避免 OVB。
- 关键词：exogenous shocks, omitted variables bias, spillovers, network, instruments。
- 定位：计量经济学因果推断方法论文。
