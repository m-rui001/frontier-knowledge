ABSTRACT（原文摘要，逐句来自正文）:
============================================================
We prove that given any β < 1/3, a time interval [0,T], and any smooth energy profile e(t) > 0,
there exists a weak solution v of the three-dimensional incompressible Euler equations such that
v is Hölder continuous with exponent β in space-time and its kinetic energy equals e(t) for all t.
Moreover, we show that a suitable h-principle holds in the regularity class C^β. The implication is
that the dissipative solutions we construct are, in a sense, typical in the space of subsolutions,
as opposed to isolated examples.

KEY CONTENT（正文，出处层级：论文正文）:
============================================================
- 问题：三维不可压 Euler 方程 ∂_t v + v·∇v + ∇p = 0, div v = 0 的弱解与能量耗散。
- Onsager 猜想（1949）：Hölder 指数 > 1/3 的弱解守恒能量；≤ 1/3 时可能耗散。
- 结果：对任意 β<1/3、任意光滑正能量剖面 e(t)，构造 C^β 的弱解使其动能恰好等于 e(t)
  ——即能耗散（能量不守恒）；并证明在 C^β 类中 h-原理成立。
- 意义：耗散解在子解的相应空间里是"典型的"，而非孤例；基本解决 Onsager 猜想的耗散一侧。
- 方法：凸积分（convex integration）与 h-原理的刚性/柔性二分。
- 定位：纯数学、偏微分方程领域，四位顶尖数学家合作。
