ABSTRACT（原文摘要，逐句来自正文）:
============================================================
Atherosclerosis is driven by the expansion of cholesterol-loaded 'foamy' macrophages in the arterial
intima. Factors regulating foamy macrophage differentiation and survival in plaque remain poorly
understood. Here we show, using trajectory analysis of integrated single-cell RNA sequencing data
and a genome-wide CRISPR screen, that triggering receptor expressed on myeloid cells 2 (Trem2) is
associated with foamy macrophage specification. Loss of Trem2 led to a reduced ability of foamy
macrophages to take up oxidized low-density lipoprotein (oxLDL). Myeloid-specific deletion of Trem2
showed an attenuation of plaque progression, even when targeted in established atherosclerotic
lesions, and was independent of changes in circulating cytokines, monocyte recruitment or
cholesterol levels. Mechanistically, we link Trem2-deficient macrophages with a failure to
upregulate cholesterol efflux molecules, resulting in impaired proliferation and survival. Overall,
we identify Trem2 as a regulator of foamy macrophage differentiation and survival.

KEY CONTENT（正文，出处层级：论文正文）:
============================================================
- 背景：动脉粥样硬化由动脉内膜中负载胆固醇的"泡沫"巨噬细胞扩张驱动；调控其分化与存活的
  因素了解不足。
- 方法：整合单细胞 RNA 测序数据的轨迹分析 + 全基因组 CRISPR 筛选。
- 发现：Trem2（髓系细胞触发受体 2）与泡沫巨噬细胞特化相关；Trem2 缺失使泡沫巨噬细胞
  摄取氧化低密度脂蛋白（oxLDL）能力下降。
- 关键结果：髓系特异性敲除 Trem2 减缓斑块进展，即使在已形成的斑块中靶向也有效，且独立于
  循环细胞因子、单核细胞募集或胆固醇水平的变化。
- 机制：Trem2 缺失的巨噬细胞无法上调胆固醇外流分子，导致增殖与存活受损。
- 定位：Trem2 是泡沫巨噬细胞分化与存活的调控因子。
