ABSTRACT (原文摘要，逐句来自正文):
============================================================
The choice of which LLM to use involves a trade-off between performance and cost. More powerful models are effective but expensive; less capable models are more cost-effective. We propose several efficient router models that dynamically select between a stronger and a weaker LLM during inference, aiming to optimize the cost-quality balance. We develop a training framework leveraging human preference data and data augmentation. Evaluation on widely-recognized benchmarks shows our approach significantly reduces costs—by over 2 times in certain cases—without compromising response quality.
