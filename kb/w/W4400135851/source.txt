ABSTRACT（原文摘要，逐句来自正文）:
============================================================
XAI methods have emerged to convert the black box of ML models into a more digestible form.
These methods help communicate how the model works, with the aim of making models more
transparent and increasing end-user trust in their output. SHAP and LIME are two widely used XAI
methods, particularly with tabular data. In this commentary, we discuss the way the
explainability metrics of these two methods are generated and propose a framework for
interpretation of their outputs, highlighting their weaknesses and strengths.

FULL TEXT KEY CONTENT（正文，出处层级：论文正文）:
============================================================
- 类型：commentary/perspective，非新算法——分析 SHAP 与 LIME 两种事后解释方法的指标如何生成，
  提出一个解释其输出的框架，点出强弱。
- 背景：ML 在生物/医学/经济/教育成功，但复杂性带来理解障碍——模型如何工作、为何做某决策、
  哪些特征最影响输出、对结果有多大把握。
- 关键词：XAI、assumptions、interpretability。
- SHAP：基于 Shapley 值（合作博弈论），给每个特征分配对预测的边际贡献；加性归因。
- LIME：Local Interpretable Model-agnostic Explanations——在单个预测的邻域拟合一个可解释的
  局部代理模型。
- 两者特别常用于表格数据。
- 主张：解释方法本身带着假设，输出需要放到框架里解读，不能当成模型"真实理由"的直接读数。
